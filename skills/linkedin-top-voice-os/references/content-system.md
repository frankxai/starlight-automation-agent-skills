# LinkedIn Content System

Use this reference when the task asks for strategy, calendars, daily posting systems, media sourcing, or performance review.

## Pillars

1. Agentic operations: how agents, humans, repos, and workflows coordinate.
2. Tool stack economics: when Codex, Composio, Make, n8n, MCP, Postiz, Taplio, or native tools win.
3. Workflow teardowns: before/after diagrams, prompts, approvals, and failure modes.
4. Taste and execution: what makes AI work feel excellent instead of generic.
5. Founder field notes: what was tried, what failed, what changed, and what is now true.
6. Product bridges: frankx.ai offers, audits, templates, workshops, and production systems.
7. Safety and governance: human gates, source receipts, platform rules, and private-data boundaries.

## Daily Rhythm

- Morning signal: one strong thesis from a current market, product, or founder-ops signal.
- Midday system: one useful teardown, checklist, stack map, or implementation lesson.
- Evening field note: one human, reflective, or taste-led post that builds trust.

Avoid three posts that sound like the same idea in different clothes. Each should earn a different reader behavior: think, save, or reply.

## Team Packet

Create the kind of packet a serious creator team would hand a top public voice:

1. Source radar with official URLs and dates.
2. Social reference from X, YouTube, LinkedIn, or credible creator commentary.
3. Video/reference link to study or cite.
4. GIF or original motion-loop concept.
5. Carousel or infographic brief.
6. Static card concept.
7. Two or three post drafts.
8. Design direction and visual QA notes.
9. Claim, platform, and license risk labels.
10. Approval route and production next action.

Do not treat social references as assets to steal. Use them to learn the pattern, cite when referenced, and create original analysis.

## Topic Lanes

For FrankX-style AI operations education, use a weighted portfolio:

- Hermes/Starlight applied agent ops as the owned authority core.
- OpenAI Codex/Codex app as the strongest public builder education lane.
- Claude Code, Projects, and coworking patterns for comparison and operator workflow lessons.
- Notion AI/workspace agents when the story connects to knowledge work, meetings, docs, team memory, or business ops.

Cover all lanes over a week, not all lanes every day.

## Post Formats

- Decision memo: "We chose X over Y because..."
- Stack map: "The clean architecture is..."
- Teardown: "Here is how this workflow should actually run..."
- Taste critique: "The output is not bad because it is AI. It is bad because..."
- Field note: "I used to believe X. Building the system changed my mind."
- Source-aware commentary: "This announcement matters less for the feature and more for..."
- Offer bridge: "If your team has this failure mode, the fix is..."

## Viral Pattern Rule

Use viral posts, videos, and GIFs as research material only:

- capture the pattern
- cite or credit the source when referenced
- change the domain, analysis, examples, and artifact
- add original expertise
- never imply the source content is yours
- never reupload copyrighted media without permission or a compatible license

Classify each asset as native reshare, licensed use, permission request,
original remake, or reject before production.

## Asset Sourcing

Prefer:

- original diagrams, screenshots, and deterministic carousels
- generated visuals that pass a visual quality gate
- licensed stock photos or videos
- official product screenshots when the license and context allow
- embedded links to source material rather than copied media

Every asset candidate needs source URL, license note, intended use, and risk label.

For media-radar tasks, also include source, platform, signal, velocity or
qualitative reason, topic lane, usage route, approval status, and a FrankX
remake brief.

## Design Style

Aim for crisp, sleek, confident education:

- source chips and dated receipts
- annotated screenshots where allowed
- stack maps and workflow diagrams
- command-center panels
- before/after operating systems
- deterministic carousel text layouts
- original short motion loops

Avoid generic robot imagery, unreadable generated text, overbusy slides, and copied viral formats.

## Scorecard

Score each candidate out of 100:

- specificity: 25
- originality: 20
- utility: 20
- credibility: 15
- taste: 10
- conversion bridge: 10

Drafts under 80 need revision. Drafts under 70 should be rebuilt from the thesis.

## Approval Packet

Include:

- title
- audience
- intent
- full post
- alternate hook
- visual plan
- source links
- claim-risk label
- platform-risk label
- CTA
- suggested publish window
- approval status
- next production action
