# LinkedIn Top Voice OS

## Overview

Use this skill to build a credible LinkedIn authority engine that favors original expertise, useful operating detail, strong design, and professional trust over engagement hacks. It can design strategy, source radar, daily team packets, approval workflows, asset sourcing, carousel/video/GIF briefs, and automation routing.

This skill can help optimize for LinkedIn Top Voice-style credibility, but never promise the badge. Treat Top Voice as an outcome of consistent expertise, originality, platform safety, and public usefulness.

## Operating Rule

Create original work from current signals. Do not copy, rewrite, or reupload another creator's content as the user's. If a post, video, GIF, or image inspired the angle, cite or credit it, extract the pattern, and make a new artifact with the user's own analysis.

Human approval is required before posting, scheduling, sending DMs, spending money, changing permissions, or publishing on any public channel.

## Daily Workflow

1. Gather current signals from primary or credible sources, including official release notes, product docs, official social/video posts, and reputable creator commentary.
2. Review the user's recent drafts, approvals, objections, and performance notes when available.
3. Produce a Top Voice Team Brief:
   - source radar summary
   - one X, YouTube, LinkedIn, or creator reference worth studying
   - one video/reference link
   - one safe GIF or original motion-loop concept
   - one carousel or infographic brief
   - one static card concept
   - design direction and visual QA notes
4. Produce two or three post candidates:
   - founder POV: a crisp thesis with original judgment
   - tactical teardown: a useful workflow, stack map, or implementation lesson
   - strategic signal: a market, tool, taste, or operations observation
5. For each candidate include hook, full draft, alternate hook, source links, claim-risk label, visual concept, carousel/video variant, CTA, and edit notes.
6. Add a legal asset scout: stock/source links, suggested generated-media prompts, and any required attribution or license notes.
7. Route the packet through approval. Draft-only unless the user explicitly approves public action.

## Automation Routing

Use the automation stack deliberately:

- Codex automations: recurring research, judgment, strategy, draft packets, review notes.
- Composio: managed-auth app actions when the agent needs to create drafts across Notion, Google Docs, Canva, Typefully, LinkedIn, YouTube, or similar tools.
- Make.com: fast front-office glue such as intake forms, notifications, CRM updates, or simple approvals.
- n8n: durable approval workflows, retries, run receipts, dedupe, private data handling, and reusable templates.
- Postiz, Typefully, Taplio, or native platform tools: optional scheduling and analytics surfaces. Do not make them the strategy brain.

Use Composio for draft creation and tool execution, then let n8n or Make own approval state. Keep publishing human-gated.

## Output Contract

When designing or running the system, return:

- content pillar or campaign
- audience and intent
- three draft posts or a dated calendar
- evidence and source links
- visual/media plan
- legal and platform-risk notes
- tool owner and fallback tool
- approval gate
- storage location for state and receipts
- next production action

## Quality Bar

Score important drafts before approval:

- specificity: concrete operating detail instead of vague AI optimism
- originality: a Frank/user point of view, not recycled consensus
- utility: reader can make a better decision or action
- credibility: sourced claims, clear caveats, no fake certainty
- taste: professional, sharp, generous, and not spammy
- conversion: natural bridge to the user's product, offer, or reputation

If a draft fails specificity or originality, rewrite before polishing.
